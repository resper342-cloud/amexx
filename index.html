Personal Loans Frequently Asked Questions

What are American Express® Personal Loans? ▼

American Express Personal Loans are unsecured personal loans that can be used for personal, family, or household expenses. You can use them for consolidating existing credit card debt, making major home improvements, funding large purchases, or major life events.

How much can I borrow and what are the repayment terms? ▼

Eligible applicants can apply for loans from $3,500 up to $40,000 (and up to $50,000 for pre-approved Card Members). Repayment options include 12, 24, 36, 48, or 60 months with predictable fixed monthly payments.

Are there any origination fees or prepayment penalties? ▼

No. There are zero origination fees, zero application fees, and zero prepayment penalties. You may pay off your loan early at any time without incurring any penalty.

Will checking my offer affect my credit score? ▼

No. Checking for a personal loan offer involves a soft inquiry that will not affect your credit score. If you are approved and accept the loan agreement, a credit review may be reported.

How quickly will I receive my funds? ▼

Once approved and your loan agreement is digitally signed, funds are typically sent via direct deposit to your bank account in as fast as 1 to 2 business days.

Is there anything I cannot use my loan funds for? ▼

Personal loans may not be used for postsecondary education expenses, real estate purchases, business purposes, securities/investments, to pay down balances on American Express-issued cards, or any purpose prohibited by law.
